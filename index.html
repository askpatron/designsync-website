FAQ

Got questions? We’ve got answers. Here’s everything you need to know about working with us.

How does it work?

Pick a plan and pay, and your login is created the moment the payment lands. Sign in, send a request, and a project manager reviews it, puts the right designer on it, and gets it back to you in a typical 24 to 48 hours. New here and not ready to commit? Describe what you need first and we will send you an invoice. Work starts once it is paid.

What counts as one design?

One request is one finished design. An Instagram post, a flyer, a poster, a business card, a roll-up banner, a certificate, an invitation card, a price list, a menu, or a presentation cover each count as one. These are counted separately, because each is its own piece of work: a flyer and its Instagram version, the front and back of a flyer, a post and its story version, and each slide of a carousel.

What if I put two things in one request?

Your project manager will ask you to split it, so each design is tracked on its own. It costs you nothing. A slot is only used when a request is accepted, not when you send it, so you are never punished for asking.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions refine the approved design direction. A new concept, changed brief, or different deliverable may count as a new request. Your workspace shows the revision allowance attached to your plan.

How fast will I get my design?

A typical 24 to 48 hours after we receive a complete brief. It is a service target rather than a countdown clock because complexity, missing content, feedback and out-of-scope work can affect delivery.

Do unused designs roll over?

No. Each month's designs are for that month, so pick the size that matches what you actually publish. It keeps the price honest and stops you paying for a backlog you will never use.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your portal. Your plan renews monthly until you do. If a renewal payment fails you get a 3 day grace period, and your plan keeps working while you sort the card out.

What is not included?

Branding and logo design, motion graphics and animation, website or app design and development, 3D work, copywriting, and printing. These are not included in the graphics plan. Your project manager flags anything out of scope before work starts.

What files do I get?

PNG, JPG and PDF, ready to use, with commercial use rights. Editable source files (AI or PSD) are ₦5,000 per design if you need them.

How do I pay? Is it secure?

Paystack handles every payment. We never see or store your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Before work starts, yes, less the payment gateway's fee. Once a designer has started, no. If we miss an agreed turnaround without telling you first, you get a free design credit or a partial credit on your account.

Why not just hire a freelancer?

A freelancer is one person with one skill set, and no cover when they are busy or offline. You get a managed team, a project manager who owns your requests, and structured briefs that keep your brand consistent piece to piece. Priced like a freelancer, run like a studio.
