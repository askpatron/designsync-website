[[IP_TERMS]]
  OPEN QUESTION FOR THE OWNER, NOT FOR PUBLICATION.
  The pricing document grants "commercial use". That is a licence, not a transfer
  of ownership, and the two are different things. A business buyer will eventually
  ask "do I own this?" and the honest answer today is "you have the right to use
  it commercially, we retain the copyright."
  Decide which you are selling:
    (a) A commercial licence, which is what the wording above describes. It is
        cheaper for you and normal for a subscription service. Say it plainly.
    (b) Assignment of copyright on full payment. This is what most clients assume
        they are buying. Under section 28 of the Copyright Act 2022 an assignment
        must be in writing and signed to be effective, so this clause alone would
        not do it. It would need to be handled properly.
  Do not leave this ambiguous. Ambiguity here is what disputes are made of.
