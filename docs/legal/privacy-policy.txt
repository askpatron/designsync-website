DESIGN SYNC | PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This policy explains how Design Sync handles personal information on the
marketing site at designs.trydesignsync.com and the client portal at
app.trydesignsync.com.

The data controller is Design Agency Sync Service Limited (RC 8159745), trading
as Design Sync, of 5 Kwaji Close, Ventures Park, Maitama, Federal Capital
Territory, Abuja. Contact us at hello@trydesignsync.com.


1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you visit the marketing site and do nothing else, the site itself does not
record a visitor profile. Your browser may request font files from Google Fonts,
which can see your IP address and that a page on our site was loaded. We do not
run advertising pixels or third-party analytics on the marketing site.

When you use public pages on the portal domain (including onboarding, package
pages, legal pages, payment pages, and chat), we may record the page viewed, the
referring site, and a one-way visitor identifier. That identifier is a hash of
your session or IP address. We do not use it as your portal account identity.
Signed-in portal visits are recorded against your account and business.

When you begin a purchase or onboarding form, we collect your name, email
address, phone number, business name, selected package, and the legal-document
versions you accepted. We also record the acceptance time, IP address, and
browser user agent as evidence of consent. We send a one-time login code to
verify your email. You may also submit a first design brief, including text and
any voice note, video, or other files you attach.

When you pay, Paystack processes your payment. We retain the payment reference,
amount, status, and account email. We do not receive or store your full card
number.

When you use the portal, we process your account and business details, hashed
password, session information, IP address, browser information, portal activity,
requests, briefs, comments, support messages, uploaded files, invoices, and
electronic-signature records. If you create a share link, we store the token
needed to serve that file without a login.

If you choose to open live chat, the widget loads our own chat on
app.trydesignsync.com. It does not load until you choose to open it. We then
process the messages you send, any name or email you provide, the page you came
from, a private continuation token, your IP address, and browser information.
Guest continuation links expire after 30 days. If you choose WhatsApp instead,
that conversation is handled by WhatsApp (Meta), not by this chat.

If you submit a testimonial, we collect your name, title, company, rating,
comments, and your consent to publish them.

If you use a referral or partner login, we process the partner contact details
and codes needed to operate that programme.

For staff and contractors, we may also hold role, employment type, start date,
and compensation records for administration and payroll.

We may display completed work in our portfolio and marketing unless you ask us
in writing to keep it private, as described in the Terms of Service. Ask us
before publication if a design contains personal data that should not appear
publicly.

Please do not include sensitive personal data in a design brief unless it is
strictly necessary and you have a lawful basis to provide it.


2. WHY WE USE INFORMATION

We process information to:

  provide the package or subscription you bought and deliver designs;
  create and secure accounts and portal sessions;
  take and reconcile payments;
  send invoices, receipts, login codes, continuation links, and request
  notifications;
  keep evidence of the terms and privacy policy accepted;
  respond to support and chat messages;
  publish testimonials you consented to share;
  understand use of our service and improve it;
  prevent abuse and investigate security incidents; and
  meet tax, company, employment, and other legal obligations.

Our lawful bases under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 include performing
our contract with you, complying with legal obligations, our legitimate
interests in operating and securing the service, and consent where we
specifically request it (including opening chat, publishing a testimonial, and
any other consent we ask for).

Transactional email about your account, invoices, login codes, and requests is
part of providing the service. It is not marketing.


3. WHO RECEIVES INFORMATION

We do not sell personal information or share it for another company's
advertising. We use service providers where needed to operate Design Sync,
including:

  Paystack, for payment processing (Nigeria);
  Resend, for transactional email (United States);
  Google Fonts, where the marketing site requests font files (United States);
  Cloudflare, for DNS, TLS, and reverse-proxy protection (global network); and
  the virtual private server we operate, which hosts the application, database,
  and files.

Each provider receives only the information needed for its service.

If you contact us on WhatsApp, Meta processes that conversation under WhatsApp's
own terms and privacy policy.

We may disclose information where law requires it, including a valid court order
or lawful request from a regulator. If the business is sold or reorganised,
information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.


4. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

The marketing site sets no first-party cookies of its own.

The portal uses cookies required for sign-in, session security, CSRF protection,
and an optional "remember me" choice. The session cookie lasts 120 minutes of
inactivity. These are necessary for requested portal functions.

If you open live chat from the marketing site, the chat loads in a frame on
app.trydesignsync.com and may set those same portal cookies on that domain so
the conversation can continue.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics cookies, or information
collected by the application for third-party advertising.


5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Some service providers process information outside Nigeria, including Resend and
Google in the United States, and Cloudflare on a global network.

The United States is not the subject of an adequacy decision by the Nigeria Data
Protection Commission. Where required, we rely on an available lawful transfer
mechanism under Part IX of the Act, including that the transfer is necessary to
perform our contract with you, and we assess the provider's contractual and
security protections.

If you would rather not have a font request go to Google, you can block
third-party font requests in your browser. If you would rather not use chat or
WhatsApp, do not open them.


6. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

We retain account, business, payment, and tax records for as long as you are a
client and afterwards for as long as Nigerian tax and company law require us to
keep records of what we sold and to whom.

Design files and request material are kept according to the expiry set when they
are stored. The default period is configured in our operations settings and can
be changed per file. Files marked permanent are kept until deleted. Expired
files are deleted automatically unless a legal hold applies.

Consent records are kept so we can show what was accepted. If we delete a client
account through our administrative process, related consent and visit records
tied to that account are removed with it.

Login codes expire after 10 minutes and are stored as hashes. Guest chat
continuation tokens expire after 30 days.

Portal and public page-visit records are not currently auto-deleted. They remain
while we operate the service, unless removed as part of an account deletion or
other lawful process.

When a retention period ends, information is deleted or anonymised unless a
legal hold or other lawful requirement applies.


7. SECURITY

Passwords and one-time login codes are stored as hashes rather than readable
text. The sites use HTTPS. Portal files are stored behind application
authorisation except when a user deliberately creates a share link.

A share link grants access to whoever possesses it. Send it only to intended
recipients and revoke it when it is no longer needed.

No system is completely secure. If a personal-data breach requires notification,
we will notify affected people and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as
required by section 40 of the Act.


8. YOUR RIGHTS

Subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may ask us to:

  confirm whether we process your information and provide a copy;
  correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  delete information;
  restrict or object to processing;
  provide portable information where applicable; or
  withdraw consent where consent is the basis.

Send requests to legal@trydesignsync.com from the email address on the account,
or include enough detail for us to verify that you are the person concerned. We
respond within 30 days. The request is free unless it is repetitive or
excessive, in which case we will tell you before charging anything.

There is no self-service deletion button. A person handles deletion by hand.
Some records may be retained where law requires it, including invoices and tax
records, or where needed to establish or defend legal claims. We will tell you
what was kept and why.

We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about
clients solely by automated means. You may complain to the Nigeria Data
Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.


9. CHILDREN

Design Sync is a business service and is not intended for children. We do not
knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have
done so, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.


10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Each published version is numbered and dated. We will provide appropriate notice
if a material change affects how we process your information.
