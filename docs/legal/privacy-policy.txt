1. WHAT WE COLLECT

When you visit the marketing site, and do nothing else:
  The page you looked at, and the site you arrived from
  A visitor identifier, which is a one way hash and is not your IP address
  Nothing else. There is no advertising pixel and no third party analytics.

When you start a purchase or an onboarding form:
  Your name, your email address, your phone number
  Your business name
  Which plan you chose
  The terms and privacy versions you accepted, the time you accepted them, your
  IP address, and your browser's user agent string. This is the record that you
  agreed, so it is deliberately more than we collect elsewhere.

When you pay:
  Paystack takes the payment. Your card number never reaches us and we never
  store it. We keep the payment reference, the amount, and your email address.

When you have an account and use the portal:
  Your login email and a hashed password. We cannot read your password.
  Your session, which stores your IP address and your user agent
  The pages you visit in the portal, recorded against your account and your
  business. This is not anonymous. We know it is you.
  Your requests, your briefs, your comments, and the files you upload

When you open the chat:
  Nothing happens until you click. The chat is provided by Tawk.to and its script
  is not loaded until you ask for it. Once you do, Tawk.to collects what you type,
  your IP address, and its own cookies. See section 4.

If you work for us, or with us, as staff or a contractor:
  We also hold your job title, your employment type, your start date, and your
  pay. That is held for employment and payroll, and is seen only by the people who
  need it.

We do not ask for and do not want your health data, your religion, your political
views, your biometrics, or anything else the Act treats as sensitive. Please do
not put any of it in a design brief.


2. WHY WE HOLD IT, AND ON WHAT BASIS

Under section 25 of the Act we need a lawful basis for each purpose. Ours are:

  To provide the service you bought, to take payment, and to deliver your designs.
  Basis: performance of a contract with you.

  To send you service email: invoices, receipts, one time login codes, and
  notifications about your requests.
  Basis: performance of a contract. These are not marketing and you cannot opt out
  of them while you have an account, because they are how the service works.

  To keep records of what you agreed to and when.
  Basis: our legitimate interest in being able to show what was agreed, and our
  legal obligation to keep business records.

  To understand which pages people use, so we can improve the service.
  Basis: our legitimate interest in running and improving our own business. We
  have kept this deliberately small: no third party analytics, no advertising
  network, no profile of you built for sale or for targeting.

  To answer you when you open the chat.
  Basis: your consent. Clicking the chat button is the consent, and it is why the
  chat loads nothing until you click.

  To keep the service secure and to investigate abuse.
  Basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the service and our clients.

4. COOKIES

The marketing site sets no cookies of its own. No banner, because there is nothing
to consent to.

The portal sets these, and it needs all of them to work:
  designsync-session   keeps you logged in during a visit. 120 minutes.
  XSRF-TOKEN           stops a hostile site submitting forms as you.
  remember_web_*       set only if you tick "remember me" at login. Long lived.

There are no advertising cookies and no tracking cookies anywhere on either site.

If you open the chat, Tawk.to sets its own cookies to recognise you across
messages. Those are Tawk.to's, and its policy is at tawk.to/privacy-policy. That
is the whole reason the chat loads on click and not on page load: nothing of
Tawk.to's touches you unless you ask for it.


5. SENDING DATA OUTSIDE NIGERIA

Resend, Tawk.to and Google are in the United States, so some of your data is
processed there. Part IX of the Act covers this.

The United States is not currently the subject of an adequacy decision by the
Commission. We rely on section 43(1)(b) of the Act: the transfer is necessary to
perform the contract we have with you, or, for the chat, on your consent under
section 43(1)(d). We have satisfied ourselves that each of these providers is a
serious operator with published security practices and its own data processing
terms.

If you would rather not have your data go to any of them, do not use the chat, and
tell us at [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] so we can talk about the rest.

7. SECURITY

Passwords are hashed, never stored as text. The site is served over HTTPS. Your
design files are not on a public URL: they sit in private storage on our server
and are served only to people the portal has authorised.

The exception is a share link, which you create yourself when you want to send a
file to someone without a login. That link is the permission. Anyone who has it
can open the file. Send it carefully.

No service is perfectly secure, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling
something. If we ever have a breach that is likely to harm you, we will tell you
and the Commission, as section 40 of the Act requires.


8. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the Act you can:

  Ask what we hold about you, and get a copy
  Have something wrong corrected
  Ask us to delete your data
  Ask us to stop or restrict what we do with it
  Object to processing we do on the basis of legitimate interest
  Ask for your data in a portable form
  Withdraw consent, where consent is the basis. For the chat, that is your browser
  settings and asking Tawk.to.

To use any of these, email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]. We answer within 30 days. It is
free, unless a request is repetitive or excessive, in which case we will tell you
before charging anything.

Being straight with you about deletion: there is no button for this yet. Ask us by
email and a person does it by hand. That is a real commitment and we honour it,
but it is manual, so it takes days rather than seconds. Some things survive a
deletion request: your consent record, and any invoice we are legally required to
keep. We will tell you exactly what was kept and why.

We do not make any decision about you by automated means. No algorithm decides
your price, your priority, or whether we work with you. People do.

If we get this wrong, tell us first and give us the chance to fix it. If we do not
fix it, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.
